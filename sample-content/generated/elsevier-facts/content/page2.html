The Brand: Color and Motto

Orange

If you've spent any time in Elsevier products — including the component library this very demo app is built with — you've seen it: orange is the color most closely associated with the Elsevier brand, used consistently across buttons, badges, and highlights in the design system.

"Non Solus"

Elsevier's logo carries a Latin motto: "Non Solus". Translated, it means "Not Alone."

The phrase points at a simple idea behind scholarly publishing: knowledge grows through connection — no single researcher, reviewer, or reader works in isolation. The published record is built by many people relying on each other's work.
